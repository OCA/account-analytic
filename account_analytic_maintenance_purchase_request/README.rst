This module propagates the analytic distribution configured on the
maintenance equipment to purchase orders linked to maintenance requests.

**Table of contents**

.. contents::
   :local:

Usage
=====

When a purchase order is linked to a maintenance request, the analytic
distribution defined on the maintenance equipment is propagated to the
purchase order lines when they are created.
