This module adds analytic distribution support to maintenance equipment.

The analytic distribution stored on the equipment can be reused by other
modules, such as purchases or inventory integrations

**Table of contents**

.. contents::
   :local:

Usage
=====

Go to:

Maintenance → Equipment

Configure the Analytic Distribution on the equipment.

Other modules may reuse this distribution when creating accounting,
purchase or inventory documents.
