1.  Go to Accounting \> Customers \> Payments (or Vendors \> Payments), or
    register a payment from an invoice/bill.
2.  Set the *Analytic Distribution* (available on the payment and on the
    register payment wizard).
3.  Confirm the payment.
4.  Open the related journal entry: the counterpart line (receivable/payable
    account) carries the analytic distribution set on the payment.
